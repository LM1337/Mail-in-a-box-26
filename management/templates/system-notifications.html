Munin alerts

Munin watches the disks, memory, load and services of this box. When a watched value reaches its warning or critical limit, or is back to normal, Munin sends one mail for each change (not every five minutes while a warning lasts). Open Munin

A plain address: letters, digits and _ + - . before the @, no name or quotes.

“Critical only” mails only the changes of plugins with a critical value and lists only those values; warnings and the return to normal are not mailed.

That is where these mails go: complaints to abuse@, mail to postmaster@, hostmaster@ and admin@ of all your domains (unless you set such an address by hand under Aliases), and the mail of root and of cron jobs. If nobody reads that mailbox, complaints about spam from your domains, for example, go unnoticed.

Tip: look at that mailbox regularly, or set up a forwarding filter in its webmail (Settings > Filters, action “Redirect message to”).
