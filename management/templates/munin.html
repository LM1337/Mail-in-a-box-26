Alerts

Munin watches the disks, memory, load and services of this box. When a watched value reaches its warning or critical limit, or is back to normal, Munin sends one mail for each change (not every five minutes while a warning lasts).

A plain address: letters, digits and _ + - . before the @, no name or quotes.

“Critical only” mails only the changes of plugins with a critical value and lists only those values; warnings and the return to normal are not mailed.
